Frequently asked questions

What are PLR and MRR products?

They are digital products supplied with rights that can vary by product. Open the individual license before using, changing or reselling an item.

Do all products have the same license?

No. The product-specific license controls. The catalogue displays a license badge and links to the License Guide.

How do downloads work?

This frontend prototype demonstrates the flow. Live downloads will require a verified membership and secure, authorized file delivery.

Can I cancel a subscription?

Subscription and cancellation handling will be managed through the payment portal once billing is connected. See the draft Refund Policy for the intended framework.
