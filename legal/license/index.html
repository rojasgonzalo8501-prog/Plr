License Guide

The individual product license always controls.

This guide helps members interpret the catalogue badges. It is a framework, not a replacement for the terms supplied with an individual product.

PLR

Private-label-rights products may allow editing, branding or resale, but the exact permissions, attribution and distribution limits differ by product.

MRR

Master-resale-rights products may include resale rights. They do not automatically grant permission to edit, claim authorship or pass on additional rights.

Commercial and personal use

Commercial-use and personal-use labels are narrower rights categories. Check the specific product before commercial publication, client work or resale.

Member responsibility

Members must read and follow the license bundled with or linked from each item. Do not represent that every product has identical rights.
